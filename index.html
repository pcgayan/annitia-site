About

Annitia Abeysuriya is a fine artist. Her work begins by hand and ends with her signature: no two pieces are made on a production line, and none leaves the studio unsigned.

Artworks

Original art, calligraphy and hand-made pieces: one-of-one works, small numbered editions and the occasional repeat of a favourite form, each made to be lived with. The first collection is on its way.

The signature

Every piece is signed Annitia A., the A. for Abeysuriya. It is how you know it was made, and approved, by her.
