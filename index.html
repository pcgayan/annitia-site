Annitia Abeysuriya

Hand-made art and ceramics, shaped, glazed and fired by the artist and signed one by one. The first collection is on its way.

The artist

Annitia Abeysuriya is a fine artist. Her work begins by hand and ends with her signature: no two pieces are made on a production line, and none leaves the studio unsigned.

The work

One-of-one pieces, small numbered editions and the occasional repeat of a favourite form, each made to be lived with: on a shelf, on a table, in the light of an ordinary room.

The signature

Every piece carries her name. It is how you know it was made, and approved, by her.
